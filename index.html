About the numbers

Built-in foods use typical USDA values per 100 g. Scanned products come from Open Food Facts, a free community database, so double-check anything that looks off. Label reads copy the printed Nutrition Facts. Claude lookups, screenshot imports and filled-in micronutrients are estimates. Net carbs = total carbs − fiber. Daily Values follow FDA labeling for adults.
